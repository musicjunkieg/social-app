Flask Privacy Policy

Effective date: 5 November 2026

Flask is an experimental client for Bluesky. It is not made or endorsed by Bluesky Social PBC.

Flask has no servers

Flask does not run any servers of its own and stores nothing on any server operated by its developer. Everything the app does happens on your device or through the services of Bluesky described below.

Your account and posts

When you sign in, your account, posts and other data are sent to Bluesky Social PBC and to the Personal Data Server (PDS) that hosts your account. That data is handled under Bluesky’s privacy policy.

Feature settings

Flask asks Bluesky’s feature-flag service which features to turn on. This request includes your account ID (your DID) and some attributes about the app.

Other Bluesky services

Flask also uses these Bluesky services:

  • Location: an IP-based lookup of your country and region.
  • App configuration.
  • Live events.
  • Age assurance.

Analytics and crash reports

Flask does not send analytics or crash reports to Bluesky or to anyone else.

TestFlight

Flask is distributed through Apple’s TestFlight. Apple collects crash reports and any feedback you submit, under Apple’s own terms.

Push notifications

Push notifications are not supported in this version.
